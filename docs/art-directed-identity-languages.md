# Repositioning fxlab around few constants, many variations

fxlab should be repositioned as a place to explore and find identity languages built from a few fixed constants and a bounded space of variation, not as a catalogue of effects or presets. The strongest sourced pattern across studio work is that the recognisable look is carried by one to three named parameters tied to a concept, not by a filter stack: Field.io's IBM Quantum system runs on three physics-derived parameters ([Field.io](https://field.io/work/ibm-quantum-brand-visuals)), and 113 Spring on colour, movement and light ([Field.io](https://field.io/work/113-spring-generative-brand-expressions)). Technically, most image looks reduce to a handful of primitives (tone maps, pattern-threshold quantisers, neighbourhood filters, coordinate warps, generators), and most transitions reduce to one matte field, a threshold and an edge style. The clearest gap in the source material is thin studio evidence: only Field.io, Collins, Studio Dumbar/DEPT, Figma's in-house team and a few others were verified, and the visual-language taxonomy below is largely synthesis with sourced examples attached where they exist. The exploration-tool literature supports lock, mutate, history and side-by-side comparison, and warns that examples fixate. On portability, the only high-fidelity extraction is a versioned recipe plus a small replay runtime; LUTs, CSS filters and Lottie carry only fragments. Rendering should be frame-indexed, seed-hashed and resolution-relative, with bit-exactness promised only per GPU class. Every claim below is marked as sourced or inferred.

Studios named in the brief but not verified in depth include Locomotive, ManvsMachine, Buck, Mother Design, Order, Spin, Ordinary Folk, Hello Monday, Bureau Borsche and Superunion; none of them supports a claim here.

## Textures and transitions share a small set of primitives

**Textures and image treatment.** The notes converge on six primitive classes; this grouping is my classification, not a sourced one, though each class is backed by sourced techniques. Point operations cover levels, curves, threshold, gradient map and blend modes. Pattern-threshold quantisers all compute `step(threshold(x,y), tone)` where the threshold field changes: Bayer maps are generated with simple bitwise operations and look structured ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ordered_dithering)), blue-noise thresholds remove low-frequency content and keep detail without visible artefacts ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ordered_dithering)), and halftone is the same idea with a dot-distance field. That makes halftone, ordered dither, stochastic screens, line and ring screens and ASCII one family with one pattern selector, a consolidation the notes propose but no source states. Neighbourhood filters include blur, edge, emboss, mosaic and Kuwahara; the anisotropic Kuwahara gives sharper edges and oil-paint flow while staying temporally coherent ([Kyprianidis](https://www.kyprianidis.com/p/gpupro/)). Coordinate warps include displacement, swirl, kaleidoscope, chromatic aberration and glitch slices. Generators include fbm, Worley and curl-style flow fields. Stateful passes include feedback, fluid, reaction-diffusion and pixel sort.

Composites are chains of those primitives. The sourced chains are riso (separate into one to three inks, screen each, multiply-overprint, offset for misregistration, add paper grain ([Effect.app](https://effect.app/effects/risograph); [chuwd19/overprint](https://github.com/chuwd19/overprint))), film (tone curve, print S-curve, halation, grain, then process variants such as bleach bypass ([Pixel Tools](https://pixeltoolspost.com/blogs/resolve/film-emulation-explained))) and VHS or CRT (chroma bleed, head-switch band, tracking, then scanlines and phosphor ([Modulate](https://modulate.to/effects/vhs/); [Vayce](https://vayce.app/tools/crt-screen-effect/))). Photocopy, Y2K chrome and iridescent foil recipes in the notes are unsourced inference. Several primitives were never researched with sources: Worley in an image-treatment context, curl noise, reaction-diffusion, Truchet, kaleidoscope, feedback, stippling, 3D LUTs and emboss.

Details that decide whether a look reads as authentic are sourced and worth encoding as module defaults. Halftone dot radius should follow `maxRadius * sqrt(coverage)`, sampling should happen at the cell centre, and antialiasing should use `fwidth` ([Heckel](https://blog.maximeheckel.com/posts/shades-of-halftone/)). CMYK screen angles are 15, 75, 0 and 45 degrees, chosen so that black sits on the least visible diagonal and 30-degree offsets give stable rosettes ([Wasatch](https://www.wasatch.com/htmlHelp/en/color_separations/moire.html)). Riso tools distinguish amplitude screens (fixed grid, mark size varies) from frequency screens (same-size marks, density varies) ([chuwd19/overprint](https://github.com/chuwd19/overprint)). Real grain is heavier in shadows, applied in scene-linear space and reseeded each frame ([StraySpark](https://www.strayspark.studio/blog/blender-film-look-halation-grain-gate-weave)). Error diffusion (Floyd-Steinberg, Atkinson) is an area operation that cannot run in a fragment shader, so a WebGL riso tool computes it on the CPU and uploads a texture ([chuwd19/overprint](https://github.com/chuwd19/overprint)). Colour handling matters too: storing linear values in 8 bits bands the shadows ([NVIDIA GPU Gems 3](https://developer.nvidia.com/gpugems/gpugems3/part-iv-image-effects/chapter-24-importance-being-linear)), and dithering must be gamma-aware because 127.5/255 is about 22 per cent linear light ([Nayuki](https://www.nayuki.io/page/gamma-aware-image-dithering)).

Tool mental models are weakly sourced. Unicorn Studio ships 70-plus WebGL effects and lets an effect act on everything beneath it or on one layer ([Unicorn Studio](https://www.unicorn.studio/docs/getting-started/)); Paper Shaders splits generators from image filters with shared scale, rotation, offset and pixel-ratio props ([Paper Shaders](https://shaders.paper.design/)); Lygia organises GLSL into 13 function categories ([Lygia](https://lygia.xyz/)). Photoshop, After Effects, TouchDesigner and Blender groupings were recalled, not verified. My suggested two-axis model, by mechanism and by pipeline position, is inference: screening before optics (halation on dots reads as ink glow), grain and paper last, dither last of all in output space.

**Transitions.** Editorial grammar is small and old: cut, dissolve, wipe, match cut, whip pan and smash cut, with Murch's Rule of Six ranking emotion and story above spatial continuity ([StudioBinder](https://www.studiobinder.com/blog/walter-murch-rule-of-six/)). The gl-transitions repository lists roughly 106 shaders (the listing was truncated) clustered into directional wipes, zooms and blurs, shape reveals, tiles and grids, flips, displacement warps, luma mattes, pixelation, glitch, light and exposure, and colour fades ([GitHub listing](https://api.github.com/repos/gl-transitions/gl-transitions/contents/transitions)). Its contract is pure and stateless: two samplers, `progress` from 0 to 1 and an aspect `ratio`, with parameters as commented-default uniforms ([gl-transitions](https://github.com/gl-transitions/gl-transitions)). That makes every shader a function of (A, B, progress, params), which is why easing and holds belong on the host.

The notes' central proposal, labelled inference, is that transitions are primitives of the form matte field × threshold × edge style. The sourced code supports each lever. `luma` and `displacement` accept any texture as the matte ([luma.glsl](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/gl-transitions/gl-transitions/master/transitions/luma.glsl); [displacement.glsl](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/gl-transitions/gl-transitions/master/transitions/displacement.glsl)). `luminance_melt` derives its matte from the outgoing frame's luminance with a threshold ([luminance_melt.glsl](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/gl-transitions/gl-transitions/master/transitions/luminance_melt.glsl)). `directionalwarp` warps both images by the matte so the seam has physical push ([directionalwarp.glsl](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/gl-transitions/gl-transitions/master/transitions/directionalwarp.glsl)). Substituting a Bayer, blue-noise or halftone threshold for `smoothstep` at the edge would yield printed-looking reveals, but no studio example of that was found. A mechanism-based taxonomy (mix, geometric matte, transform, field-driven matte, resample or distort) gives shared parameters of direction, softness, count and seed, and gallery clutter such as heart, star, cube and page-curl shapes can be treated as trend-driven, though that judgement is unsourced. For motion tokens, Material's emphasised easing is `cubic-bezier(0.2, 0, 0, 1)` ([m3.material.io](https://m3.material.io/styles/motion/easing-and-duration/tokens-specs), via a search summary, so verify) and Apple parameterises springs by response and damping fraction ([Apple](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/swiftui/animation/spring(response:dampingfraction:blendduration:))).

## Studios define constants first, then hand over generators or cookbooks

The sourced handover forms range from templates to running tools. Studio Dumbar/DEPT delivered League of Legends MSI as a "cookbook" of templates permitting controlled variation ([It's Nice That](https://www.itsnicethat.com/features/forward-thinking-motion-design-morphing-the-next-era-graphic-design-150125)). Field.io's IBM Quantum work shipped a generative brand tool exposing its three physics parameters, and it produced per-attendee badges and invites from location data ([Field.io](https://field.io/work/ibm-quantum-brand-visuals)). The Adidas system plugs into the client's existing Scala CMS so seasonal content arrives through familiar channels ([Field.io](https://field.io/work/real-raw-fast-identity-system)). Connor Campbell cites custom browser systems that let brands generate real-time motion assets themselves, naming Spotify Wrapped and Canvas ([The Brand Identity](https://the-brandidentity.com/insight/how-to-meaningfully-incorporate-motion-into-branding-with-studio-dumbar-dia-and-connor-campbell)). A creative coder selling such tools self-reports a three-phase process, EUR 10-30k and two to six months ([Nahuel Gerth](https://nahuelgerth.de/thoughts/brands-in-motion-the-future-of-generative-tools)); that is a seller's figure, not an independent one. Figma built its dither and lo-fi tools in Figma Make for Config 2026, which is the nearest analogue to what fxlab could hand over ([Figma](https://www.figma.com/blog/the-visual-identity-behind-config-2026/)).

How the "few constants" are chosen is the most transferable finding. Hübner's survey of dynamic identities says opening at least one identity element gives a brand a "living character" inside predetermined guidelines, and recognisability survives "like a playlist" ([Patrik Hübner](https://www.patrik-huebner.com/datadesigndictionary/dynamic-identities/)). MIT Media Lab shows the constant clearly: one 7x7 grid, one monogram, per-group variations ([Pentagram](https://www.pentagram.com/work/mit-media-lab)). Collins' Muse Group work calls the goal "flexible consistency" across four apps sharing "visual genetics" ([It's Nice That](https://www.itsnicethat.com/articles/collins-muse-group-graphic-design-project-220725)). Tyler Hobbs's flow-field essay shows the same idea at algorithm scale: five or six meaningful controls (grid resolution around 0.5 per cent of width, step length 0.1 to 0.5 per cent, curve length, angle quantisation, seed-point strategy), where short curves give fur and long curves give fluid lines ([Hobbs](https://www.tylerxhobbs.com/words/flow-fields)). Quantising angles to multiples of pi/4 or pi/10 gives "sculpted, rocky" forms, a single parameter with a strong stylistic effect ([Hobbs](https://www.tylerxhobbs.com/words/flow-fields)). Reas's Software Structures produced 26 pieces from written instructions in the manner of Sol LeWitt ([Whitney](https://whitney.org/exhibitions/software-structures)), a model for a look defined as instructions plus ranges.

Sourced cautions concern process rather than parameters. Mitch Paone argues that "Hiring animators to animate design does not work" and that motion must be designed with the identity ([The Brand Identity](https://the-brandidentity.com/insight/how-to-meaningfully-incorporate-motion-into-branding-with-studio-dumbar-dia-and-connor-campbell)). In data-driven work, Bremer's practice relaxes the need to explain and leads with visual appeal while alluding to data trends ([Visual Cinnamon](https://www.visualcinnamon.com/about/)). My inferences, not sourced: name parameters for the concept ("superposition") rather than the effect ("blur amount"); apply one consistent treatment to all supplied imagery to make it feel owned; and generic gradient-and-grain sameness is the likely failure mode, though no source documents failure cases, seed conventions or range-limit practice. For data mapping, Bertin's variables and the Cleveland-McGill ranking suggest quantities go to position, length or value and categories to hue, shape or texture ([Information Visuals](https://www.informationvisuals.com/information-design-theory/elementary-perceptual-tasks)); Vega-Lite's field-plus-type encoding is the cleanest binding model to copy ([Vega-Lite](https://vega.github.io/vega-lite/docs/encoding.html)).

## Exploration needs lock, mutate, compare and honest portability

The creativity-support literature backs a specific toolkit. Shneiderman's principles include exploring alternatives with easy backtracking, keeping history to record, compare and revert, and exploratory search ([Shneiderman](https://www.cs.umd.edu/~ben/papers/Shneiderman2006Creativity.pdf), snippet only, PDF unreadable). Resnick adds "wide walls", multiple pathways from floor to ceiling, and a Scratch natural experiment found causal evidence that widening walls raised engagement ([Resnick](https://mres.medium.com/designing-for-wide-walls-323bdb4e7277); [Dasgupta](https://medium.com/hci-design-at-uw/testing-the-wide-walls-design-principle-in-the-wild-bbb3487fbbb3)). Karl Sims's 1991 work and Picbreeder established selecting and breeding results, with Picbreeder adding publishing and branching from others' images ([Sims](https://www.karlsims.com/papers/SimsSiggraph91.pdf); [Secretan et al.](https://dl.acm.org/doi/10.1145/1357054.1357328)). Design Galleries frames the two hard problems as dispersion (choose parameter vectors whose outputs differ) and arrangement for browsing ([Marks et al.](https://dl.acm.org/doi/abs/10.1145/258734.258887)), and later work such as Design Adjectives uses a few examples to steer a gallery instead of sliders ([Design Adjectives](https://dl.acm.org/doi/10.1145/3379337.3415866)). Example fixation is documented: people shown an example reproduce its features even when its flaws are pointed out ([Leahy et al.](https://dalyresearch.engin.umich.edu/wp-content/uploads/sites/237/2020/12/Leahy_Daly_McKilligan_Seifert-Design-Fixation-From-Initial-Examples-Provided-Versus-Self-Generated-Ideas.pdf), snippet). The notes found no controlled study of presets versus a blank slate for effects tools, so applying this evidence to fxlab is inference.

On seeds versus looks, Midjourney is the sourced cautionary model: a seed reproduces an image only with an identical prompt, and its own documentation lists seeds under "Not for Saving Styles", pointing to style-reference codes instead ([Midjourney](https://docs.midjourney.com/hc/en-us/articles/32180011136653-Style-Reference)). Keeping seed (exact output) and named look (transferable to new content) as separate objects follows directly.

On what survives elsewhere, the sourced facts are these. A 3D LUT answers "given this colour, what colour" and cannot carry grain, halation, bloom or vignetting ([Arc](https://www.welcometoarc.com/post/true-film-emulation-building-a-photochemical-pipeline), snippet). Colour-only operations bake accurately; spatial, temporal and texture-adding effects do not, and 33-point cubes lose accuracy against formulas ([Nobe LutBake](https://timeinpixels.com/nobe-lutbake/)). Lightroom presets cannot contain LUTs and conversions between formats lose features ([Adobe community](https://community.adobe.com/questions-729/converting-photoshop-lightroom-xmp-preset-to-premiere-cube-luts-1629933), snippet). Lottie silently drops most After Effects effects, blending modes and luma mattes, and runtimes differ ([LottieFiles](https://help.lottiefiles.com/supported-after-effects-features)). CSS and SVG filters are limited, and the Safari claim comes from older documentation and may be stale ([A List Apart](https://alistapart.com/article/finessing-fecolormatrix/)). The working precedent for high fidelity is Paper Shaders, which ships a zero-dependency runtime with props ([GitHub](https://github.com/paper-design/shaders)), and Unicorn Studio, which exports a JSON config for a roughly 36kb gzipped runtime ([Unicorn Studio](https://www.unicorn.studio/docs/embed/), snippet). Substance Designer's "exposed parameters" let others use a graph without understanding its internals ([Adobe](https://helpx.adobe.com/substance-3d-designer/substance-model-graphs/exposing-parameters.html)), the closest precedent for a found language with three to six headline controls.

## Deterministic export depends on hashing, fixed timesteps and honest limits

For video, WebCodecs `VideoEncoder` plus a JavaScript muxer such as Mediabunny is frame-accurate ([Mediabunny](https://mediabunny.dev/guide/introduction)), while Chrome's MediaRecorder writes live-stream WebM without duration or cues and delivers frames in real time, dropping them if encoding lags ([Remotion](https://www.remotion.dev/docs/webcodecs/fix-mediarecorder-video)). Field data from 1.1 million sessions says H.264 and VP9 are effectively universal for WebCodecs encoding while AV1 and HEVC are not ([WebCodecs Fundamentals](https://webcodecsfundamentals.org/datasets/codec-analysis-2026/)). `VideoEncoder` discards alpha by default and must be told `alpha: "keep"` ([MDN](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/API/VideoEncoder/configure)); alpha video works as VP9 WebM in Chrome but Safari needs HEVC with alpha ([Chrome for Developers](https://developer.chrome.com/blog/alpha-transparency-in-chrome-video)), so PNG sequence remains the universal alpha route. YouTube's recommended upload is H.264 High profile MP4 with BT.709 tags and, for 1080p at 24-30 fps, 8 Mbps ([YouTube Help](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/1722171)). Social specs came only from aggregators, not platform pages.

For stills and print, the sourced limits are hard. WebGL guarantees only 4096 for texture and renderbuffer size, and iOS Safari enforces a WebGL heap of roughly 300-500 MB ([WebGL Fundamentals](https://webglfundamentals.org/webgl/lessons/webgl-cross-platform-issues.html); older, mixed-quality sources). Canvas stores premultiplied alpha, so `getImageData` round trips quantise low-alpha pixels, and `canvas.toBlob()` is the safer export path ([dev.to](https://dev.to/yoya/canvas-getimagedata-premultiplied-alpha-150b)). Canvas 2D supports `display-p3` and `float16` ([MDN](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/API/HTMLCanvasElement/getContext)). The notes verified nothing about PDF/X, ICC embedding, CMYK or spot-colour PDF. My inference, unverified, is that no browser API produces CMYK and the honest print offer is RGB plus grayscale ink separations with conversion left to the print shop's RIP.

For determinism, sourced facts are that trig, pow and sqrt can differ between GPUs and that texture filtering precision is hardware-specific ([WebGL Fundamentals](https://webglfundamentals.org/webgl/lessons/webgl-precision-issues.html)). Playwright baselines are named per browser and OS and must be generated where they are compared ([Playwright](https://playwright.dev/docs/test-snapshots)). The practical rules the notes derive are inference from training knowledge: hash (seed, integer coordinate, frame) with integer PCG-style hashes in GLSL ES 3.00 rather than `fract(sin(dot))`, which gl-transitions itself uses ([CrossZoom.glsl](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/gl-transitions/gl-transitions/master/transitions/CrossZoom.glsl)); set time from frame index over fps; express radii and sample counts relative to output size; keep loops to whole cycles; record schema version, seed and build in the project JSON. Promise bit-exact output only per GPU and driver class, and "visually identical within tolerance" otherwise. Not researched: ffmpeg.wasm, GIF or APNG encoders, ProRes, OpenColorIO, cross-GPU difference magnitudes, and pixelmatch or SSIM specifics.

## Conclusion

The most useful reframing is that fxlab's asset is not its module count but its constraint vocabulary. The verified studio work shows the ownable part of an identity is a small, concept-named parameter set inside a fixed skeleton; fxlab's opportunity is to let a designer discover that parameter set by exploring, then freeze it. That points at consolidation (fewer, more general primitives with pattern and matte selectors) more than at growth in effect count, and at treating seed, lock and branch as core objects, not conveniences.

The largest caution is fidelity honesty. A found language is only fully reproducible inside a versioned runtime on similar GPUs; every other format loses either space (LUTs), texture (CSS), effects (Lottie) or exactness (cross-GPU floats). A portability report that says this up front, together with a stronger evidence base for the visual families than these notes provide, is the precondition for extraction to be credible. The next research pass should collect real identity projects for the print-simulation, glassy and liquid families, and verify Rive, After Effects and guideline-platform handover practice.
